Voice & messaging

Our voice is how we sound, and our messaging is what we say. This chapter gives everyone who writes for Beautiful.ai 1 story and 1 voice to tell it in.

Voice

Our voice

We sound like a trusted colleague who has built a thousand decks.

Foundation sets our personality as the Working Sage. Our voice is how that personality sounds in words. We bring wit when it helps the point land and drop it when it gets in the way.

Plainspoken

We say it straight and cut every word that doesn't help professionals get their work done. We use their own vocabulary and start from what they already know.

checkPaste your notes and get a first draft of your deck in minutes.
closeUnlock next-level storytelling with our revolutionary AI-powered solution!
Declarative

We promise an outcome and state it with confidence. We say the direct thing once and let it stand, so professionals know exactly what they get.

checkYour deck is on brand before you share it.
closeBeautiful.ai may help you create decks that could look more professional.
Empathetic

We write to professionals with respect, the way a trusted colleague would. We understand the problem behind their work before we suggest solutions.

checkBoard meeting tomorrow? Start from your outline and keep your evening.
closeStill building slides by hand? It's time to get with the program.

Tone

Our voice never changes. Our tone turns up or down with the room.

A product label and a launch post come from the same voice. The label has room for 3 words at low volume, and the post has room for a story at high volume.

Messaging

Positioning

We are the presentation platform for professionals.

Professionals present for a living, and their decks stand in for their credibility. The default slide tools ask them to design by hand, and AI-first tools hand them decks they can't send to a client. We sit between the two. AI drafts the story with them, and Smart Slides lay out every slide as they work.

Positioning statement

For professionals who present for work, Beautiful.ai is the presentation platform that turns ideas into work-ready decks in minutes.

Presentation platform

Brand promise

Work ready.

We took a product descriptor, work-ready presentations, and dropped the hyphen to turn it into a promise to professionals. Work ready speaks to the person: ready for the work and ready for the room. With the hyphen, work-ready describes what they make and the product they make it in.

Work readyThe promise

No hyphen. It speaks to professionals and calls them to the work. Use it on its own or as a headline.

Work ready.

work-readyThe descriptor

Hyphenated. It describes the decks professionals make and the platform they make them in. Use it before a noun.

Work-ready slides in minutes.

The beautiful wayThe frame

A frame, never a headline. It always sits above a work-ready claim.

The beautiful way to build a deck. Work-ready slides in minutes.

Boilerplate

Use this paragraph as written.

It closes every press release and partner page. Shorten it freely, and never add a claim it doesn't make.

Boilerplate

Beautiful.ai is the presentation platform for modern professionals. It combines design automation and AI to help anyone create on-brand, work-ready presentations in minutes. Professionals at over 100,000 companies use Beautiful.ai to pitch and present. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in San Francisco, Beautiful.ai is backed by Trinity Ventures, First Round Capital and General Catalyst.

Proof

Every claim has a source.

We use numbers our customers reported and quotes from our published customer stories, always with the person's name and company. A number without a source stays out of the copy, however good it sounds. Our scale line is “Trusted by professionals at over 100,000 companies,” the same number the site uses.

Writing

Writing rules

These 3 rules catch our most common slips.

They hold for headlines, product labels, sales decks and this guide. When a draft breaks one, rewrite the sentence instead of patching the word.

Never hedge

A guideline that hedges isn't a guideline. We state it and let it stand.

checkBuild your sales deck in minutes.
closeBeautiful.ai can potentially help speed up your deck workflow.
Break the triad

3 parallel fragments in a row is our old habit. We rewrite it every time.

checkStart from your outline, and Create with AI builds the first draft.
closeFaster. Smarter. More beautiful.
Name the customer

We put professionals and their work in the sentence. They are never users or folks.

checkSales leaders send on-brand pitch decks without waiting on design.
closeUsers can generate on-brand outputs.

Mechanics

We write in sentence case and use numerals for every number.

Headings, buttons and labels all use sentence case. We write 3 slides, never three slides. We use no emoji and no exclamation marks in brand or product copy, and we keep em dashes rare.
