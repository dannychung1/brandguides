Motion

We use motion to help professionals get to know our product and to build excitement around what's possible with our platform.

How we move

We hold every animation to 6 principles, and the best work meets all of them at once.

Emotion

We move to give professionals the feelings they want from their work, such as the excitement of a new idea or the satisfaction of a finished deck.

Competence

We move with intention, showing our mastery and command to earn trust.

Clarity

We move to feature, focus, and to deepen understanding.

Ease

We move with an air of effortlessness and comfort, so building a presentation never feels like a struggle with the tool.

Nimbleness

We move like the smart and flexible platform we are, adapting each layout as teams add and rearrange their content.

Purpose

We move only with intent. Every motion serves the story, an interaction or feedback, and we cut anything that does not.

Our logo in motion

Our logo animation shows a brand that thinks fast: smart, nimble and sure.

The wordmark resolves from soft focus to sharp in under 2 seconds, on our gradient of Agency Azul and Ready Rose. Like the rest of our motion, it moves with intent and gets out of the way.

Slides in motion

Slides move through depth as flat, rigid panels, so professionals see the range of what they can make.

Slides glide on Deepest Blue with our standard ease, and every slide stays real and legible. A frame holds 1 headline at most, and the slides never bend, warp or melt into each other.
