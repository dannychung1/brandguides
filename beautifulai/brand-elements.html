Brand elements

We use our brand shape and our icons to frame professionals' work and to guide them through our product and our stories.

Brand shape

Symbol as a frame

We use our symbol to frame photography and to compose with our other assets.

The b and the period work together as the symbol or apart as 2 shapes, always exactly as drawn. We take them far past logo size, so professionals feel the brand in the frame before they read a word.

arrow_forward add

The b

We use the b at huge scale, cropped so only its stem and bowl show. It sits inside the gradient a step lighter or darker.

The period

The period is a perfect circle. It sits behind professionals, holds copy on a soft tint, and can hold a professional in a line of type.

Cropped or in the line

At scale, both shapes run off at least 1 edge. The period appears whole only when it sits in a line of type.

The sign-off

The symbol tile signs off social posts and videos in a corner, at logo size.

Brand shape in use

The period can hold a professional, and the b never holds a photo.

These come from our shipped work. Our type and our product stay plain and utilitarian, so the shapes bring the softness, and they keep the focus on the professional.

01

Photo framing

The period frames the professional. It sits behind a cut-out portrait, holds a close shot whole in a line of type, or runs huge around a wide shot until the frame crops it. Each one puts a soft field of focus around the person.

02

Composition

The b and the period compose with our type, product and brand assets. At huge scale, a step lighter or darker than the gradient, they give the layout structure and depth while the message stays in front.

Don't

These 3 uses break the shapes, so we never make them.

The b never holds a photo, a shape never floats whole on its own, and the period always stays a perfect circle.

closeDon't put a photo inside the b. It turns the logo into a picture frame.
closeDon't float a whole shape on its own. Crop it at an edge, or set it in a line of type.
closeDon't stretch the period into an oval. It is always a perfect circle.

Iconography

Icons come from Material Symbols, outlined and lightly rounded. No emoji, no hand-drawn glyphs or whimsical illustrations.

Go to Material Symbols ↗
Style

Outlined, rounded variant, unfilled. Stroke weight 300 with no grade. Optical size matches the size the icon is shown at.

Sizes

24px is the standard. 20px in dense UI. 48px for feature icons.

Color

Inherit text color by default. Cyan only when calling for action.

Spot illustration

We use spot illustration sparingly and with intent, mainly inside the product to guide professionals through a moment such as sign-up, an empty state or a new feature. We draw it in the icon vocabulary, one step more complex.
